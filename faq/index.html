Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Trace without subscribing?

Yes. Trace Free includes six focus sessions per day, three active habits, the Classic 25/5 timer, built-in categories, day statistics, and private iCloud sync across your devices. You can also export or delete data, restore purchases, and manage subscriptions.

How does the seven-day trial work?

Eligible new subscribers can start a seven-day free trial of the Monthly Trace Pro subscription. Apple determines eligibility, and each Apple Account can redeem only one introductory offer within the Trace Pro subscription group. Unless cancelled before the trial ends, the subscription renews monthly at the price Apple displays when you confirm the purchase. Annual and Lifetime do not include this trial.

What does Trace Pro add?

Trace Pro adds unlimited focus sessions and habits, advanced and custom timers, reminders, custom categories, detailed history and statistics, session notes, widgets, App Intents, and Live Activities. iCloud sync is included for everyone.

How does lifetime access work?

Lifetime access is a one-time, non-consumable Apple in-app purchase. It unlocks the same Trace Pro entitlement without an expiration or automatic renewal.

Will an expired subscription trap my running timer?

No. An existing active session can always be paused, resumed, and stopped. Trace then returns to the Free limits without deleting your data.

Does Family Sharing apply?

No. Family Sharing is not enabled for Trace Pro at launch.

How do I cancel?

Open Trace → Settings → Trace Pro → Manage Subscription, or use Apple’s subscription settings. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase and has no recurring payment to cancel.
