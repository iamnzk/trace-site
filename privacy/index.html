Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Trace is designed to keep your productivity data under your control. This policy explains what the app processes and why.

App data

Focus sessions, habits, goals, categories, settings, and profile choices are stored on your device and synchronized through your private Apple iCloud account using CloudKit, for Free and Pro users. Apple processes that data under your Apple account and privacy terms.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and billing. Trace uses RevenueCat to determine whether the anonymous app customer has an active Trace Pro entitlement from a subscription or lifetime purchase. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, product identifiers, purchase history, subscription status, trial status, renewal and expiration information, platform, and diagnostic events needed to provide purchases and analytics. Trace does not send RevenueCat your name or email and does not call its identity login APIs.

Apple Health and widgets

With your permission, Trace reads selected HealthKit measurements to display habit progress. These measurements are held in memory and queried again from Apple Health; they are not written to Trace’s synced database or preferences. Derived habit widget summaries use a protected, reconstructable App Group cache excluded from device backups. Widgets can read this cache after you first unlock your device following a restart. Old preference copies are discarded on upgrade. Trace cannot remove copies already present in older device backups. Disconnecting a Health habit or clearing Trace data removes its local cached progress; Apple Health originals remain in Apple Health.

Focus Protection (Screen Time)

If you turn on Focus Protection, Trace uses Apple’s Screen Time framework to block the apps and websites you choose, only while a focus session (and, if you choose, its breaks) is running. Apple gives Trace your choices as opaque tokens: Trace cannot see which apps or websites they are, or how you use them. The selection stays on this device in Trace’s App Group; it is not synchronized to iCloud or sent anywhere. You can remove Trace’s Screen Time access at any time in Settings.

Profile photo

A profile photo you choose is stored with your other Trace data and synchronized through your private iCloud account. Trace only reads the photo you pick; it has no access to the rest of your photo library.

Recovery and deletion

Trace can preserve unreadable databases and pre-migration habit history in local recovery folders. Clear All Data removes those recovery copies along with custom focus profiles and local preferences. When the open store uses CloudKit, record deletions propagate asynchronously when sync is available. A local-only store does not delete existing iCloud records.

Notifications and diagnostics

Notification preferences and active-timer state are stored locally or in Trace’s App Group so the app, widgets, App Intents, and Live Activities behave consistently. Apple may provide crash and performance diagnostics through App Store Connect when your device settings permit it.

Tracking and advertising

Trace does not sell personal information, show third-party ads, or use data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Your choices

You can export app data, clear local and synced Trace data, manage subscriptions, restore purchases, and change notification permission in Settings. Clearing Trace data does not cancel a subscription or remove a lifetime purchase from your Apple Account.
