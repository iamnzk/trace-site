Make focus a practice.

Trace combines focused sessions and habits in one calm productivity app, with deeper planning, insights, sync, and system features in Trace Pro.

Free and Trace Pro

Trace Free includes six focus sessions per day, three active habits, the Classic 25/5 timer, built-in categories, day statistics, and private iCloud sync across your devices. Eligible new subscribers can try the Monthly Trace Pro plan free for seven days before it renews at the price shown by Apple. Monthly, annual, or lifetime Trace Pro unlocks unlimited sessions and habits, advanced timers, reminders, custom categories, detailed history and statistics, session notes, widgets, App Intents, and Live Activities.
