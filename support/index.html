Trace Support

Contact

Email muhdnzk@gmail.com with your iOS/iPadOS version, Trace version, device model, and a short description. Do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive notes. Expected response time: two business days.

Subscription help

Open Trace → Settings → Trace Pro. Use Manage Subscription for status, billing, or cancellation guidance. Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling or moving to a device using the same Apple Account. Apple—not Trace or RevenueCat—handles charges and refund decisions.

Free limits

Trace Free includes six focus sessions per day and three active habits. Sessions from all your devices count toward the daily limit. It uses the Classic 25/5 timer, built-in categories, day statistics, and private iCloud sync. Trace Pro removes those limits and unlocks advanced features.

Timer or Live Activity

Existing running sessions can always be paused, resumed, or stopped. Live Activities, widgets, and App Intents require active Trace Pro access.

Notifications

Check both Trace’s notification preference and iOS Settings → Notifications → Trace. Turning notifications off inside Trace cancels its pending Pomodoro alerts and clears its badge.

Data recovery

If Trace cannot open its database, use Retry first. “Reset Local Cache & Re-download” preserves the unreadable store in a recovery folder before creating a clean cache. iCloud data can re-download when available.
